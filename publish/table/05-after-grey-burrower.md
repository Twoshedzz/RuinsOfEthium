### Grey Burrower

| AC | HP | Notes |
|----|----|-------|
| 15 | 68 | Current: ____ · grappling? ____ |

| Trait | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Speed** | 30 ft, burrow 30 ft · Tremorsense (ground) |
| **Multiattack** | Bite +6 (2d10+4, Grapple escape DC 13) · Tail Slam +6 (2d8+4, DC 13 STR or Prone) |
| **Burrow** | Skip attacks → vanish → erupt within 30 ft next round |

**Safety:** too hard → worm takes prey and retreats · too easy → erupt near casters.

Once chaos hits, captain realises the outpost cannot be held. Duergar **escape** — they do not try to win.

- Captain (if alive): **"Fall back! Seal the Deep Gate!"**
- One soldier may run for the southern doors
- Crossbows: one more volley, then fall back
- Quaggoths: keep fighting unless dragged or ordered away

### Environmental actions

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Push mining cart | STR DC 12 → 20 ft; path DEX DC 12 or 1d6 + prone |
| Collapse loose rubble | Attack AC 10 / HP 10 or STR DC 13 → difficult terrain / block |
| Leap onto rubble | DEX DC 12 → high ground; off floor = harder for tremorsense 1 round |
| Lure the Burrower | Noise / meat / bang metal — no check if clever; else DC 12 |

**When the fight ends:** surviving duergar break south → Beat B. Chase through doors *before* they close = one round of pursuit, then doors win.

## Beat B — Deep Gate / spider

**Timing rule:** if the PCs do **not** witness the retreat (still fighting the worm, elsewhere, or delayed), **do not** narrate the doors yet. Wait until they **approach that corridor**. Then they catch the end of the withdrawal.

### Optional: Stone Marshal / spider (cinematic, ~1 minute)

Use when they can see the southern approach. Skip only if the fight already ate the clock *and* they never go near the doors tonight.

**Preferred colour:** a **duergar on a large cave spider** at the threshold — calm, armoured, dark steel inlaid with black Ethium, enormous stone hammer. He may dismount / stand with spiders screening the retreat. Same face as the Marshal beat in the fragment.

Without raising his voice: **"Enough."**

Spiders do **not** charge the party — they screen the Burrower / buy seconds while engineers finish the gate. The Marshal **never attacks**. When the last duergar passes through, he steps back; spiders retreat with him.

### Read aloud (doors seal)

> The great doors grind together with a thunderous boom. Thick bars drop into place one after another. Blue veins of ancient Ethium flare across the stone before fading once more.
>
> Silence.
>
> Whatever lies beyond those doors is no longer another dungeon. It is an occupied city.

**Truth for you:** forcing the doors tonight is **very hard / impossible** without keys, allies, or a plan. Name for later: **Deep Gate**. Optional DC 14 Investigation / Perception → Ethium key slot / seal mechanism (foreshadow only).

## Beat F — Return to Fallcrest (waterfall ambush)

Uneventful until the waterfall path.

### Preferred colour — the escaped / wild ogre

Broken trees · dead goblins · huge footprints · heavy chains dragged through mud. Something enormous **broke free**. A chaotic fight is already underway: goblin handlers trying to regain control, bugbears barking, archers keeping distance. **Nobody cares about the party at first.**

Keep useful PDF stats (Session 4 pack pp. 10–11). Soften “Hobgoblin King scripted ambush” — the King may still be the distant power who *wanted* a warning shot, but **tonight’s colour** is wild ogre + panicking goblinoids. **Ogre may escape** (balances the fight / leaves a recurring threat).

## Beat G — Tobbs / Fallcrest follow-up

From the ChatGPT fragment — **kids table**: mystery and worry, not gore. Do **not** invent past fragment + existing canon.

**NPCs:** [Tobbs](/dm/world/characters/tobbs/) · [Nimozaran](/dm/world/characters/nimozaran/) · [Garnel](/dm/world/characters/garnel-stoneblender/) · [Mala](/dm/world/characters/mala-stoneblender/)  
**Language sheet** (binding words only if they ask): [ethium_language_sheet.pdf](/dm/pdfs/ethium_language_sheet.pdf) — *Kaelith Vorn* = bind item ↔ stone; do **not** invent a teleport phrase tonight.

### Town arrival

Something feels wrong. Town continues as normal, but people **whisper**. Nobody has seen **Nimozaran**.

### Tobbs — empty study

Exhausted; hasn’t slept properly.

- Nimozaran **never leaves without telling him** — not once, for years
- One morning the **study door stayed locked**
- Hours later Tobbs unlocked it: room **empty**
- No struggle · fire still burned · tea on the desk · books open · quill on unfinished notes
- As though he had **simply vanished**

Do **not** confirm kidnapping. Do **not** confirm teleportation. Leave every possibility open.

### The tiefling visitor (seed)

Two days later a well-dressed **tiefling woman** visited the tower. Polite questions about: the **map**, **Ethium**, **Garnel**, the **ruins**. She smiled, thanked him, left. Tobbs wishes he had paid closer attention. *(Fits Gang of Four / Valnaran interest — do not name Maelis unless they already know.)*

### If Garnel is with the party — Mala’s notes (soft beat)

Only if **Garnel** came to Fallcrest. While they are in the study / office (looking around, comforting Tobbs, poking open books):

- Garnel recognises **grandmother Mala Stoneblender’s** hand among Nimozaran’s papers — scraps / annotated pages, not a full stolen book
- Soft implication only: **Nimozaran and Mala knew each other** before she died (shared Ethium research). No long flashback.
- One line is enough — e.g. Garnel, quiet: *"That’s… Grandmother’s writing. He had her notes."*

**DM:** seed revealed **if this scene plays**. Do not force if Garnel stayed at the pool. No answers about *how* the notes got here tonight.

### Tobbs demo — Ethium stones, Transit, and the Anchor

**If time / earned.** Aligns with fragment (*Ethium Transit Network*). Kid-friendly show-and-tell — unlock the idea of faster travel; **do not** ship the whole party to the pool tonight unless the table is buzzing and you want one supervised hop.

Tobbs leads them **beneath the tower** (Nimozaran never showed this to outsiders).

| Thing | Kid-friendly meaning | Table fact |
|-------|----------------------|------------|
| **Transit Circle** | Magic doorway floor — unfinished | Nimozaran’s **life’s work**, not ancient Ethium ruins |
| **Anchor Stone** | Fallcrest’s **home address** | Enormous blue stone **fused into the foundations**; Fallcrest was **never** on the old network — without the Anchor, the town has **no address** |
| **Transit Stone** | A small stone that **remembers places** | Already knows two destination runes: **Fallcrest** · **Ethium Pool**; new circles teach new runes later |

**Tobbs (plain speech):** *"Master said every place in the old world had an address."* He sets the little blue Transit Stone in the circle.

#### Optional 30-second demo (safe)

> The unfinished circle wakes — soft blue light crawls along the carved lines. The Transit Stone’s Fallcrest rune and Ethium Pool rune both glow. The Anchor in the floor answers with a deep, steady thrum — like a heartbeat under the tower.
>
> Tobbs grins, tired and proud. *"See? Stones for the journey. The big one stays here so home can be found. The little one learns the way."*

- If they have the **Explorer’s Compass**: needle may twitch toward the Anchor / nearest Ethium Stone — same relic idea as the viewing table, not a new system.
- Optional language flavour only if someone asks how binding works: *Kaelith Vorn* (sheet) — bind / link. No new teleport word.

If the clock is gone: plant “Tobbs has something under the tower to show you — tomorrow” and stop.

### Soft end image (optional)

If they still have the Explorer’s Compass: Garnel (or Tobbs) sets it down — needle turns **not** north, **not** toward the pool — somewhere none of them have explored yet.
