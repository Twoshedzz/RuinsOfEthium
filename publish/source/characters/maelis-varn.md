# Maelis Varn

**Third Hand** of the **Valnaran family** (tiefling trade racketeers; one of the Gang of Four).

## Campaign facts

- Author of the letter found in the ruined manor (addressed to **Rulden**, the manor mage)
- Ordered seizure of Mara’s Ethium map; kill courier if needed; deliver map to an agent at the river crossing
- Letter also implied interest in Ethium’s “old places” and (in prep / novel context) watching Tobbs
- Still at large — open thread
