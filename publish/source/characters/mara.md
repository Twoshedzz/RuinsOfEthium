# Mara

Courier from Fallcrest. Carried a map recovered from Ethium’s outer ruins, meant for **Nimozaran**.

## Campaign facts

- Ambushed by goblins on the North Road; rescued by the party (Ch. 1)
- Gave Thorn the escort’s warded shield after the cave fight
- Arranged meeting at the **Blue Moon Alehouse**
- Fallcrest locals know her; guards recognised her at Knight’s Gate
- Family: grandfather helped rebuild Lowtown after the orc war
- Homunculus watched the Blue Moon roof during the feast (unexplained — open thread)
- Maelis Varn’s letter ordered the Hammers to seize her map (and kill the courier if needed) — party got there first
