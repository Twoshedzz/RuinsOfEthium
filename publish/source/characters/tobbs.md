# Tobbs Quickfoot

Halfling apprentice to **Nimozaran**. Cheerful, untidy, often carrying papers and quills; worry shows under the patter.

## Campaign facts

- Meets the party at the **Blue Moon** after Mara delivers the Ethium map (Ch. 2)
- Explains Ethium plateau / waking stones; demonstrates a stone with **Aethra Lumis** (light and warmth)
- Directs party toward Ethium via river road → waterfall climb → ruined tower
- Pleased when they return a stone from the manor; still wants more for Nimozaran (Ch. 3)
- Manor letter (Maelis Varn) also ordered the Hammers to **spy on Tobbs**
- **Session 5 seed (if played):** can demo Nimozaran’s unfinished **Transit Circle** under the tower — **Anchor Stone** (Fallcrest’s address) + small **Transit Stone** (knows Fallcrest · Ethium Pool). See table plan Beat G.

## Spelling

Campaign / novel: **Tobbs Quickfoot**. Fallcrest gazetteer text: **Tobolar Quickfoot** — same apprentice; use Tobbs in prose.
