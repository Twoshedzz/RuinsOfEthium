# Mala Stoneblender

Garnel’s grandmother. Researched Ethium and the stones.

## Known facts (from Garnel’s notes / play)

- Made discoveries into the stones; Garnel still has scraps of her research
- Books taken / believed stolen by a mage guild; most destroyed when Garnel tried to recover them
- One remaining book was confiscated by the **Hammers** and held as ransom (forcing Garnel to work for them)
- Pool / well research and “the flow” appear in her notes (see healing-pool / secret lore links below)
- **Session 5 seed (if played):** scraps of her notes may turn up in **Nimozaran’s study** — implies she and Nimozaran knew each other / shared Ethium research before she died. Canon only if that scene lands at the table.
