# New / Early Stage Support
*(Problem framing, vision setting, and outcome definition)*
* Facilitate **structured, inclusive workshops** with senior stakeholders, policy teams, delivery teams and users to build shared understanding
* **Frame complex problems** by identifying user needs, policy intent, constraints and opportunities
* Help teams **articulate a clear service vision** aligned to strategic, policy and organisational outcomes
* Define and agree **measurable service outcomes**, ensuring alignment between user needs, policy goals and delivery priorities
* Support early decision-making by clarifying **scope, assumptions, risks and dependencies**

⠀
# Feasibility Study / Pre-Discovery
*(Reducing risk and preparing services for discovery)*
* Identify and articulate **business drivers, policy intent and delivery constraints**
* Collate and synthesise **existing user research, operational insight and performance data**
* Identify **known user needs, high-level pain points and unmet needs** across current services
* Identify **subject matter experts, service owners and key stakeholders** across Defra and ALBs
* Align proposed work to **departmental strategy, programme objectives and policy outcomes**
* Work closely with **Policy and Operational colleagues** to define:
  * the problems we are trying to solve
  * the outcomes we are aiming to achieve
* Work alongside **Business Analysts and other professions** to understand:
  * people, processes, data and technology
  * constraints within existing “as-is” services
* Create **as-is service and user journey maps**, highlighting:
  * pain points
  * dependencies
  * risks and assumptions
  * hypotheses to test
* Develop **early service and interaction prototypes** to explore options and test assumptions
* Provide **clear recommendations** on next steps, including delivery approach and design maturity
* Support teams to **make the case for a formal discovery**, grounded in evidence and risk reduction

⠀
# Existing Programmes of Work
*(Improving coherence, performance and user experience at scale)*
* Lead and align engagement with **service managers, product owners, policy teams and delivery partners**
* Define and articulate a **coherent end-to-end service vision** across complex programmes
* Visualise and map **existing services, products and processes**, identifying:
  * duplication
  * inefficiencies
  * inconsistencies
  * dependencies and failure points
* Map **relationships between products, services and platforms** across delivery areas
* Identify opportunities for:
  * **service improvement**
  * **cost reduction and simplification**
  * **better alignment between policy intent and service delivery**
* Ensure services align to **real user journeys and tasks**, not organisational structures
* Lead on **cross-service and cross-programme consistency**, ensuring interactions and journeys work together
* Track user needs and service performance **across organisational and departmental boundaries**
* Influence **performance frameworks and metrics** so we measure what matters to users and policy outcomes
* Support service owners and programme leads with **service design assurance and strategic guidance**

# New Programmes of Work
*(Shaping services from vision to delivery)*
* Create **as-is and to-be service and user journeys**, identifying risks, opportunities and hypotheses
* Develop a **strategic service vision**, grounded in user needs, policy objectives and delivery realities
* Design the **service structure, interactions and reusable building blocks** that underpin delivery
* Create and iterate **service prototypes** to test ideas, assumptions and trade-offs early
* Work with delivery teams to ensure **user needs remain central throughout delivery**
* Influence performance measures so services **demonstrably improve outcomes for users and policy**
* Lead on ensuring **coherent, joined-up user journeys** across products and delivery teams
* Provide **expert advice to Business Boards, Programme Boards and Working Groups**
* Participate in **Defra-wide business forums** to embed service design thinking and practice
* Lead **show-and-tells, learning sessions and communities of practice** to build organisational capability

# Role of Lead Service Designers (explicit call-out)
Lead Service Designers play a critical role in:
* **Shaping and influencing strategic roadmaps**, ensuring delivery plans align to:
  * user needs
  * policy intent
  * long-term service outcomes
* **Mapping products, services and capabilities** across delivery areas to show:
  * how they relate to each other
  * how they support policy outcomes
  * where there are gaps, overlaps or risks
* Acting as a **bridge between policy, strategy and delivery**, helping teams “move left” and reduce downstream risk
* Providing **assurance and leadership** on service coherence, maturity and user-centred practice at scale
