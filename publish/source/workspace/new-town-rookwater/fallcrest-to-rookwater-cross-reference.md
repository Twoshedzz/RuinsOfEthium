# Fallcrest → Rookwater Cross-Reference

This is a private migration aid for replacing Fallcrest material with the original town of Rookwater. It is not intended to appear in the published story or gazetteer.

The replacements are functional equivalents, not renamed copies. Geography, history, government, descriptions and characterisation should follow the Rookwater gazetteer.

## Core Terms

| Existing reference | New reference | Migration note |
|---|---|---|
| Fallcrest | Rookwater | Replace throughout public-facing prose |
| Original setting river | River Orrel | The Orrel flows south through Almenor and supports barge trade |
| Upper Fallcrest / Hightown | Crown Hill | A fortified ridge, not a town-dividing cliff |
| Lowtown | The Riversides | Collective term for Reedmarket, the Quays, Southbank and East Ward |
| Lord Warden | Mayor | Rookwater uses an elected civic government |
| Faren Markelhay | Mayor Celandra Vale | Former river factor; practical and approachable |
| Fallcrest Guard | Rookwater Guard | Friendly green-coated civic watch |
| Sergeant Murgeddin / Murgaddin | Captain Branna Holt | Senior friendly guard and principal watch contact |
| Bloodspear War / orc attack | Ash-Hand goblin raids | Goblin raids occurred approximately one hundred years ago |
| Fallcrest town crest | Silver rook | Usually shown as a small silver rook clasp |

## Character and Story Substitutions

| Existing use | Rookwater approach |
|---|---|
| Courier seeking the town wizard | Courier seeks Master Elian Voss at Starling Spire |
| Apprentice briefs the party | Pippa Reed meets them at the Copper Kettle or Mayor's Hall |
| Friendly veteran guard provides a lead | Captain Branna Holt requests help and provides lawful authority |
| Ruler rewards the party | Mayor Celandra Vale offers payment, civic standing or a public contract |
| Trouble in a ruined district | Use illegal excavations or sealed raid cellars in the Goblin-Ruin Ward |
| Town threatened from the northern wilderness | Use the north road, Eastwatch reports or displaced upland settlers |
| River journey toward civilisation | Barges travel south on the Orrel through Almenor |
| Ancient magical infrastructure | Use pre-Almenoran survey roads and sealed way-mark chambers |

## Terms to Remove Rather Than Rename

The following should be removed from Rookwater material unless independently rebuilt with a clearly different concept:

- Setting-specific pantheons and temple dedications
- The Markelhay family and its history
- The titles “Lord Warden” and “High Septarch”
- The original war, enemy horde and historic battle names
- Original merchant houses and proprietors
- Original numbered-map layout and distinctive geography
- Any copied location prose, boxed text, statistics, maps or artwork

## Migration Rule

Use this sheet to identify a passage's function, then rewrite the passage from the Rookwater gazetteer. Do not perform a simple name substitution on existing prose. If an old scene depends on geography that Rookwater does not share, restructure the scene around the bridge, Crown Hill roads, river wards or Goblin-Ruin Ward.
