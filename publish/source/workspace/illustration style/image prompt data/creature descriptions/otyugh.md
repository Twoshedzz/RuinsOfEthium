# Otyugh

## Prompt-ready description

An Otyugh is a foul, heavy scavenger with a swollen rubbery central body about
five feet across, supported by three thick stumpy legs. Two long muscular
tentacles end in spiked gripping suckers. A third sensory stalk rises above the
body. Its central maw is broad and powerful, lined with several rows of teeth.

## Anatomy and movement

- Exactly three supporting legs.
- Two grasping tentacles plus one sensory stalk; do not multiply or exchange
  these appendages.
- Tentacles originate from the body and remain visibly continuous.
- Heavy, low-slung body adapted to sludge and refuse.
