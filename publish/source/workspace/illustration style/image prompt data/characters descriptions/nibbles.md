# Nibbles

## Prompt-ready description

Nibbles is a small, wiry red-scaled Dragonborn or kobold rogue with a long
reptilian snout, sharp teeth, bright green eyes, swept-back ridged horns, a
spined red crest, clawed hands and feet, and a thick tapering tail. A small
cloth pouch hangs from his right horn. He wears a voluminous black hooded cloak
over fitted brown studded leather, with dark metal shoulder and knee plates,
bracers, belts and several practical pouches. He carries short daggers.

## Non-negotiable

- Nibbles is reptilian, not a goblin.
- No pointed goblin ears, human nose or bald goblin-like head.
- Preserve bright red scales, luminous green eyes, swept horns, spined crest,
  tail and clawed feet.
- Preserve the horn pouch when it can be seen at the chosen scale.
