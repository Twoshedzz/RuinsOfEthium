# Dave and Peggy

## Prompt-ready description

Dave is a tall orange-gold Dragonborn wizard with a fully reptilian dragon head,
broad scaled snout, two swept horns, smaller brow and cheek spikes, amber eyes
and layered golden scales. His robes combine burnt orange panels with deep
indigo-blue sleeves and hood, edged with geometric gold trim and secured by
practical leather belts and pouches. He carries a dark wooden spellbook and a
gnarled natural wooden staff whose forked top cradles a glowing green crystal
orb.

Peggy is his tiny coppery-gold pseudodragon familiar: a miniature four-legged
dragon with a reptilian head, leathery wings and a long barbed tail. She may
perch on Dave's shoulder when the scene calls for her.

## Non-negotiable

- Dave is never a human, lizard-headed human or masked wizard.
- Preserve his orange-gold scales, horn arrangement, indigo-and-orange robes,
  wooden spellbook and green-orb staff.
- Peggy is never an owl or ordinary bird.
- Keep staff, spellbook and limbs physically connected and readable.
