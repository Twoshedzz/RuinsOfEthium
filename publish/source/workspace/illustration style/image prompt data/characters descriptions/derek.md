# Derek

## Prompt-ready description

Derek is a stocky dwarf cleric with a completely bald pink-toned head, round
brass wire-rimmed spectacles, pale blue-grey eyes and a luxuriant copper-red
beard gathered into a short central braid. He wears heavy silver plate with warm
gold-bronze details over a deep blue tabard. His rectangular-headed warhammer
and large heater shield both carry radiant golden sun emblems.

## Non-negotiable

- Bald head, round spectacles and braided beard must remain visible when angle
  and framing allow.
- He is a dwarf, with compact proportions and a low centre of gravity.
- Hammer and shield must connect naturally to believable hands and arms.
- Preserve the copper-red beard, round spectacles, blue tabard and paired sun
  emblems.
