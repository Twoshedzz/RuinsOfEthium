# Thorn

## Prompt-ready description

Thorn is a tall, heavily muscled male Half-Orc warrior with cool pale-blue skin
that carries a violet cast and deep blue-purple shadows. His skin is never green
or olive. He has a broad, stern face, pointed ears and short, thick black hair
with close-cut sides and a rough textured top. Fine scar-like markings cross his
face and body. He wears a huge grey-brown fur mantle over dark chainmail,
weathered leather harnesses and battered plate reinforcement. He fights with a
one-handed, single-headed battleaxe and a round shield bearing the Fallcrest
emblem.

## Non-negotiable

- The axe is single-headed and used one-handed.
- Skin is cool pale blue with violet undertones, never green or olive.
- Preserve his broad facial structure, pointed ears, black hair and stern
  expression.
- The shield is worn on his other arm and never floats.
- From the front, his hand remains behind the shield rather than appearing on
  its outward face.
- From the rear or side, show a believable forearm, grip and straps.
- Never let the shield arm bend backwards or double back on itself.
