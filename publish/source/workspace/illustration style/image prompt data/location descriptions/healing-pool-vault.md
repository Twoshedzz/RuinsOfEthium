# Healing Pool Vault

## Prompt-ready description

A grand domed chamber built from smooth pale Ethium stone. At its centre stands
a circular basin carved from translucent stone, with sockets and precise carved
channels around its rim. A stone scholar's desk and ancient working surfaces
occupy one side of the chamber.

## States

- Dormant: pale colourless masonry and a dry or still basin.
- Active: Ethium Stones in the sockets awaken blue light in the designed
  channels and fill the pool with luminous water.

## Non-negotiable

- Glow follows purposeful channels and sockets; do not cover every wall in
  random blue veins.
- The chamber is built architecture with a dome, not a stalactite cave.
