# Derek

## Prompt-ready description

Derek is a stocky dwarf cleric with a completely bald crown, round wire-rimmed
spectacles and a long braided beard. He wears heavy plate armour. His signature
equipment is a warhammer and a heater shield bearing a radiant sun emblem.

## Non-negotiable

- Bald head, round spectacles and braided beard must remain visible when angle
  and framing allow.
- He is a dwarf, with compact proportions and a low centre of gravity.
- Hammer and shield must connect naturally to believable hands and arms.
