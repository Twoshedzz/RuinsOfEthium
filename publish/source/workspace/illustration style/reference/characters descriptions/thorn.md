# Thorn

## Prompt-ready description

Thorn is a tall, muscular male Half-Orc warrior with a short dark undercut,
rugged face, small lower tusks and a fur mantle over leather or chain armour.
He fights with a one-handed, single-headed battleaxe and a round shield bearing
the Fallcrest emblem.

## Non-negotiable

- The axe is single-headed and used one-handed.
- The shield is worn on his other arm and never floats.
- From the front, his hand remains behind the shield rather than appearing on
  its outward face.
- From the rear or side, show a believable forearm, grip and straps.
- Never let the shield arm bend backwards or double back on itself.
