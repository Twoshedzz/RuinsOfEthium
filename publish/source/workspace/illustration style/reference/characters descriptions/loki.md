# Loki

## Prompt-ready description

Loki is a sturdy wolfhound or hunting dog with a natural canine build and an
alert, loyal bearing.

## Non-negotiable

- A natural dog, not a wolf-monster, werewolf or armoured war beast.
- Keep normal canine leg and paw anatomy.
