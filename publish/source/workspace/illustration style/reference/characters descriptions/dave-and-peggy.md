# Dave and Peggy

## Prompt-ready description

Dave is a tall golden Dragonborn wizard with a fully reptilian dragon head,
pronounced snout, horns and visible scales. He wears long wizard robes and
carries a leather spellbook and a wooden staff tipped with an orb.

Peggy is his tiny coppery-gold pseudodragon familiar: a miniature four-legged
dragon with a reptilian head, leathery wings and a long barbed tail. She may
perch on Dave's shoulder when the scene calls for her.

## Non-negotiable

- Dave is never a human, lizard-headed human or masked wizard.
- Peggy is never an owl or ordinary bird.
- Keep staff, spellbook and limbs physically connected and readable.
