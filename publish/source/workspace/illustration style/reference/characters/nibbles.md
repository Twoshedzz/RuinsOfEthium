# Nibbles

## Prompt-ready description

Nibbles is a small red-scaled Dragonborn or kobold rogue with a reptilian snout
and cheeks, swept-back horns, bright eyes, clawed hands and a slim draconic
build. A small pouch is tied to one horn. He wears a dark hooded cloak over
studded leather gear and carries daggers.

## Non-negotiable

- Nibbles is reptilian, not a goblin.
- No pointed goblin ears, human nose or bald goblin-like head.
- Preserve the horn pouch when it can be seen at the chosen scale.
