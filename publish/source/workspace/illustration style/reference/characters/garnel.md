# Garnel Stoneblender

## Prompt-ready description

Garnel is a short, scruffy young adult gnome researcher with a wild mop of
bright red hair, round spectacles and an alert, nervous expression. He carries
leather parchment folders, an inkpot and quill, charcoal pencils, brass lenses
and small measuring instruments.

## Non-negotiable

- Young adult gnome, not a child or elderly wizard.
- Wild hair, spectacles and research equipment are his primary visual cues.
- Include only the tools relevant to the action; do not overload every scene.
