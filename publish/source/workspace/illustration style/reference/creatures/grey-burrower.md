# Grey Burrower

## Status

The Grey Burrower's final visual design is **not yet approved**. Do not treat
rejected chapter-image drafts as canonical anatomy references.

## Established facts

- Blind subterranean predator.
- Approximately 50–60 feet long.
- Approximately 5 feet in body diameter.
- One continuous limbless worm body.
- Burrows through earth and stone and can erupt through walls or floors as
  specified by the scene.
- Powerful bite and tail-slam attacks.

## Details requiring a scene or design decision

- Exact head-opening mechanism.
- Tooth arrangement.
- Surface armour and segmentation.
- Degree of resemblance to familiar worm or petalled-jaw creatures.

## Non-negotiable

- Threat comes from length, speed and bite—not building-sized diameter.
- Establish scale using people or architecture on a comparable depth plane.
- No random arms, tentacles, eyes or disconnected body parts.
- The mouth must sit at the terminal end of the continuous body.

## References

No canonical image is currently approved. External web images may be assigned
separate roles for body scale, head mechanics or surface texture. State what to
use and what to ignore for every image.
