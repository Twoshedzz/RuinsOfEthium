# Duergar

## Prompt-ready description

Duergar are compact, broad, heavily muscled dark dwarves with ash-grey skin,
pale or white beards and hard, severe faces. Their military equipment is heavy
and brutal: dark iron armour, spiked pauldrons, reinforced shields, war picks,
warhammers and crossbows.

## Anatomy and behaviour

- Dwarven proportions: short, broad and grounded.
- Equipment looks practical, weighty and subterranean rather than ornate.
- Enlarged Duergar must be explicitly requested by the scene; ordinary Duergar
  remain dwarf-sized.
