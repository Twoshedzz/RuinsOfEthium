# Goblin

## Prompt-ready description

Ethium goblins are lean, wiry humanoids with large pointed ears, sharp teeth,
long expressive hands and quick, crouched movement. They wear crude leather or
scale armour and carry jagged iron weapons.

## Non-negotiable

- Small and wiry rather than childlike.
- Distinctly humanoid; not canine or reptilian.
- Equipment should feel scavenged and mismatched.
