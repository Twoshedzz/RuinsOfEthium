# Quaggoth

## Prompt-ready description

A Quaggoth is a hunched bipedal or knuckle-walking yeti-gorilla creature covered
in shaggy pale fur. It has a broad, flat ape-like face, pointed ears, a small
chin beard, heavy shoulders, very long muscular arms, broad clawed hands and a
wide fanged mouth.

## Anatomy and behaviour

- Ape-like body plan; never canine.
- Long arms, shorter powerful legs and a forward-hunched torso.
- Natural fur and claws; no armour unless a scene explicitly adds restraints or
  equipment.
- Chained Quaggoths remain physically powerful rather than emaciated.
