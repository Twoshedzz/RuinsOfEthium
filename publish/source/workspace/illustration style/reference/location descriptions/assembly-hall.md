# Ethium Assembly Hall

## Prompt-ready description

A vast, high-ceilinged grand dwarven hall whose vaulted roof rises into
darkness. Soaring carved stone pillars frame a broad central avenue of fitted
flagstones. At the far southern end, two colossal dwarven gates seal the lower
city. Duergar occupation may add rough wooden barricades, mining carts, weapon
racks, iron spikes and chained prisoners.

## Spatial character

- Monumental architecture; people and five-foot-wide creatures remain modest in
  scale beside the pillars and gates.
- Clear floor plane and strong pillar rhythm help establish distance.
- The underlying architecture is ancient and formal; Duergar additions are
  crude, dark and temporary.

## Non-negotiable

- No stalactites.
- Do not merge figures or creatures into pillars.
- A breach or eruption point must be placed exactly where the scene brief says:
  wall and floor are not interchangeable.
