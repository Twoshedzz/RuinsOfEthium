# Otyugh Lake Cavern

## Prompt-ready description

A vast natural underground vault containing a black, stagnant lake below a
fifty-foot cliff face. A narrow worked archway opens high in the stone wall.
Collapsed ancient Ethium bridge masonry forms an island or field of rubble near
the centre of the water. The atmosphere is damp, foul and cavernous.

## Spatial character

- Natural cavern surrounding isolated remnants of precise pale Ethium
  stonework.
- Strong vertical scale from lake level to the high corridor arch.
- Dark water, wet rock and bridge rubble should remain distinct textures.
