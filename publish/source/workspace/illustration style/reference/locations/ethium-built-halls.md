# Ancient Ethium Built Halls

## Prompt-ready description

Ancient Ethium interiors are deliberately constructed monumental stone spaces:
square-cut pale blocks, smooth slabs, tall arches, geometric carving, massive
square pillars and fitted flagstone floors. Surfaces are old, cracked and
partly ruined but retain precise dwarven craftsmanship.

## Materials and light

- Building stone is pale and near-white, hard as granite and smooth as marble.
- At rest, the masonry has no glowing veins.
- Blue light appears only when an Ethium Stone actively energises carved
  channels or sockets.
- Use torchlight, magical light or deep ink shadow as specified by the scene.

## Non-negotiable

- Built halls have carved masonry ceilings, not generic cave stalactites.
- Damage should reveal blocks, joints, chips and rubble rather than turning the
  entire location into a natural cave.
