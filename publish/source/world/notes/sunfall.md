# Sunfall

Capital of **Almenor** (★ on the Axirian peninsula map). South of the heartland, on the river.

## Campaign facts

- Base of the **Gang of Four** (four criminal guilds allied here for ~4 years)
- Their power is reaching north toward Fallcrest
- Garnel was kidnapped / held here before being forced into Ethium work for the Hammers
- Plot-hook note: an Axirian spy operating out of Sunfall may employ the Gang of Four — see [`plot-hooks-session-era.md`](plot-hooks-session-era.md)
