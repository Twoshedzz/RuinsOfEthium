# The Hammers

Newer street gang within the **Gang of Four** — mostly youths; dominance through thuggery. Hammer pendants.

## Campaign facts

- Local Fallcrest cell used the **ruined manor** cellars (orc-war ruin) as a hideout
- Attacked the Blue Moon during Tobbs’s briefing (Ch. 2)
- Forced **Garnel** after confiscating Mala’s last book; held him reading runes in Ethium’s ruined tower
- May show Ethium-sickness (blue veins) in later prep / fights
- Unstable alliance with Silent Knives and the other three guilds
