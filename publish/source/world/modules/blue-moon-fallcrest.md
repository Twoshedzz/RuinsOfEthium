## Overview & Town Background

**Fallcrest** is a bustling frontier town built on two stone terraces along the Nentir River — **Hightown** (housing noble manors, temples, and the Wizard's Tower) and **Lowtown** (home to markets, docks, and vibrant alehouses).

Upon arriving at **Knight's Gate**, newcomers face inspection by Sergeant Murgaddin's city watch. Rescuing courier Mara guarantees swift entry and an invitation to a celebratory feast at the **Blue Moon Alehouse**.

At the Blue Moon, scholar **Tobbs** presents an ancient Ethium plateau map and demonstrates a glowing **Aethra Lumis** Ethium stone. The briefing is abruptly crashed by a gang of **Hammers thugs** seeking to snatch the map.

## Key Areas & Read-Aloud Descriptions

### 1 — Knight's Gate (City Entrance)

Twin granite towers flank the fortified northern archway of Fallcrest. Guards inspect incoming merchant wagons and travelers.

> **Read Aloud:** *High stone walls rise above the tree line as Knight's Gate comes into view. Guards in polished chainmail stand watch beneath the portcullis. Beside the gatehouse, Sergeant Murgaddin — a stocky dwarf with a braided iron-grey beard and sharp blue eyes — leans on his halberd, eyeing incoming travelers.*

- **Gate Inspection**: Captain Murgaddin demands to know the party's business.
- **Mara's Vouch**: If Mara is with the party, she steps forward: *"They saved my life and the King's mail on the North Road, Sergeant."* Murgaddin smiles, waives gate taxes, and grants entry.
- **Without Mara**: DC 12 Persuasion or 5 sp entry tax per traveler.

### 2 — Fallcrest Town Errands (Optional Open Exploration)

Party members can visit various shops before heading to the tavern:

- **Naerumar's Imports**: General store run by Orena Naerumar. Sells adventuring gear, climbing kits, potions of healing (50 gp), and obscure maps.
- **Half-Moon Trading House**: Weapon repairs, commission custom axes/shields.
- **The Nentir Inn**: Upscale lodging in Hightown (2 gp/night).
- **Septarch Tower**: High Wizard **Nimozaran's** steep stone tower in Hightown (guarded by magical wards; courier satchel delivered here).

### 3 — The Blue Moon Alehouse (Feast & Briefing)

A cozy two-story tavern in Lowtown known for roasted venison, spiced ale, and warm hearths.

> **Read Aloud:** *The heavy oak doors of the Blue Moon open into a warm, buzzing hall. A massive stone fireplace crackles at the far wall. The smell of roasted venison and mulled wine fills the air. Up on the timber rafters, a tiny leather-winged homunculus watches the crowd with glowing amber eyes.*

- **Mara's Feast**: Mara purchases a lavish feast for the party (fresh bread, venison stew, dark ale).
- **Tobbs's Arrival**: Scholar **Tobbs** enters, placing a heavy leather-bound journal and a cloth-wrapped stone on the table.
- **Ethium Stone Demo**: Tobbs uncovers the **Aethra Lumis** stone — a translucent azure crystal that pulses with soft blue light when touched. He displays his plateau map and explains: *"Ethium was a city of ancient magic that vanished overnight. These stones are keys to its remaining vaults."*

### 4 — The Hammers Brawl

The tavern doors slam open as armed thugs in leather vests enter.

> **Read Aloud:** *The tavern quietens as four heavy-set thugs step into the room, their knuckles wrapped in leather and iron-spiked bludgeons at their belts. Their leader, a scarred fighter with a crimson bandana, steps toward Tobbs's booth. "Hand over the blue rock and the map, old man, and nobody gets hurt."*

- **Combat Goal**: The Hammers want to steal the Ethium stone and map, not murder the tavern patrons.
- **Encounter**: 3–4 Hammer Thugs + 1 Hammer Bruiser (Leader).
- **Environmental Actions**:
  - *Tavern Table Shove*: DC 12 Strength to flip a table for half-cover.
  - *Tankard/Chair Toss*: Improvised weapon attack (1d4 bludgeoning).
- **Guards Arrive**: On Round 4, Sergeant Murgaddin and 4 city guards burst in; surviving thugs flee into the alleyways.
