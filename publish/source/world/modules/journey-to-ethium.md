# Module — Journey to Ethium

**Status:** Ready to run. Bridges manor / Fallcrest → ruined tower / pool.

**Canonical PDFs:** [`session3_stat_blocks_landscape_a4.pdf`](../../../table-assets/pdfs/session3_stat_blocks_landscape_a4.pdf) → `/dm/pdfs/session3_stat_blocks_landscape_a4.pdf` · waterfall return contingency: [`Ruins_of_Ethium_Session4_DM_Pack_FULL.pdf`](../../../table-assets/pdfs/Ruins_of_Ethium_Session4_DM_Pack_FULL.pdf) pp. 10–11

## Overview

Overland from Fallcrest to the plateau (~two days on the road), then Tobbs’s **waterfall** climb onto Ethium, then first plateau sights before the ruined tower.

---

## Areas

### 1 — River / road (≈ two days)

- **Night:** wolf attack at camp
- **Burial mounds:** axe beak herd rushes and attacks
- **Watch:** a cloaked figure can be mistaken for a rock and flee — the party is being followed

### 2 — Waterfall climb

Tobbs’s route: treacherous stone path **behind the falls**. If the party splits (steeper right vs longer safer path), the climb gets harder.

- Goblin archers from above; **bugbears** come down
- Sleep can stop arrow fire; ledge fights with bugbears; fleeing goblins at the top

### 3 — Plateau approach (non-combat set dressing)

Collapsed **healing shrine** · distant **black dragon** · half-goblin drop · **castle on a hill** · **goblin wolf riders** on a ridge (non-combat) · head for ruined tower Tobbs marked.

---

## Encounters & stats

### Wolves (night)

CYOA `river-wolves`: **3–4 wolves**. **No AC/HP line** on Session 3 sheet — use open wolf rules.

### Axe beaks (burial mounds)

CYOA `axe-beak-herd`: **3–5 axe beaks**. Hard; Sleep can drop some.

| Creature | AC | HP | Attack | Notes |
|----------|----|----|--------|-------|
| Axe Beak | 11 | 17 | Beak +4, 1d8+2 | Fast and aggressive — Session 3 sheet |

### Waterfall climb

CYOA `waterfall-climb`: goblin archers + **2 bugbears**. Hard when the party is split.

| Creature | AC | HP | Attack | Notes |
|----------|----|----|--------|-------|
| Goblin | 15 | 7 | Scimitar/bow +4, 1d6+2 | Nimble Escape — Session 3 sheet |
| Bugbear | 16 | 27 | Morningstar +4, 2d8+2 | Heavy hitter — Session 3 sheet |

*(Session 4 pack waterfall return uses Bugbear AC 16 HP 27 Morningstar +5 2d8+2 · Goblin Archer AC 13 HP 10 — prefer Session 3 sheet for the original climb; pack numbers if running the later ambush.)*

### Contingency — waterfall ambush if returning to Fallcrest later

From Session 4 DM pack / [`05-after-grey-burrower.md`](../../../table/05-after-grey-burrower.md). Prefer **wild escaped ogre** colour (chains, dead goblins, fight already underway; ogre **may flee**) over a pure scripted Hobgoblin King ambush — keep PDF stats either way.

| Creature | AC | HP | Attack / role |
|----------|----|----|----------------|
| Ogre | 11 | 59 | Greatclub +6, 2d8+4; Rock +6, 2d6+4 — may escape if bloodied |
| Hobgoblin Captain | 17 | 39 | Longsword/Longbow +4, 1d8+2; Leadership +1d4 — optional officer; retreats if losing |
| Bugbear ×2 | 16 | 27 | Morningstar +5, 2d8+2; Ambusher +2d6 on surprise |
| Goblin Archer ×6 | 13 | 10 | Shortbow +4, 1d6+2; Nimble Escape |
