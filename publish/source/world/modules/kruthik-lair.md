# Module — Kruthik Lair (Brood Nest & Queen)

**Status:** Ready to run. Optional tactical side-dungeon for 2nd-level adventurers seeking Ethium stones and ancient relics.

## Overview & Dungeon Background

Tucked beneath the ruins of Tower C on the Ethium plateau lies a tight 3-room subterranean nest carved out by **Kruthiks** — chitinous insectoid predators attracted to the raw magical energy of unrefined Ethium Stones.

Garnel Stoneblender can mark the location of the nest from rumors overheard while captive. The dungeon has a distinct **Aliens survival-horror feel**: narrow 2.5-foot crawlspaces, skittering claws in darkness, pulsating egg sacks, and an aggressive Kruthik Queen defending her brood nest.

> **DM Secret — Plateau Tunneling**: The rampant tunneling of kruthiks and Grey Burrowers across the plateau is the actual cause of the eastern pool cave-in (DC 12 check to spot clawed side-tunnels while excavating the pool rubble).

## Key Areas & Read-Aloud Descriptions

### Room 1 — Swarm Tunnels

Narrow stone passages pocked with burrow holes and covered in fine grey dust.

> **Read Aloud:** *The tunnel shrinks to a tight stone passage barely three feet wide. The rock walls are riddled with smooth, claw-carved holes. Dust drifts down from the ceiling as a frantic, multi-legged skittering echoes from all directions.*

- **Movement Hazard**: Passages are tight (2.5 to 3 ft wide); Medium creatures treat this area as difficult terrain.
- **Encounter**: 5–8 Young Kruthiks.
- **Tactics**: Start with 5 on the board. Kruthiks emerge from wall holes to surround PCs using *Pack Tactics*. If PCs advance easily, bring in 2 more from rear holes on Round 2.

### Room 2 — The Brood Chamber

A humid subterranean cavern filled with wet, translucent egg clusters.

> **Read Aloud:** *Humid, foul-smelling air fills a wider cavern. Hanging from the ceiling and coat-rimming the stone floor are translucent egg sacks glowing with pale blue light. Four-legged chitinous horrors with razor mandibles crouch over the brood, clicking their pincers in threat.*

- **Encounter**: 2 Adult Kruthiks + 3 Young Kruthiks.
- **Egg Pressure Rule**: If an area spell or attack strikes an egg cluster, or after 2 rounds of combat, **1d2 Young Kruthiks hatch** from the sacks. Egg clusters have **AC 10, HP 5** — destroying them prevents hatching.

### Optional Side Vault — Explorers' Crypt

A quiet side alcove containing ancient dwarven coffins webbed over with kruthik secretions.

> **Read Aloud:** *A low-ceilinged crypt branches off the brood chamber. Broken stone coffins lie covered in sticky web strands. Wrapped in webbed cocooning against a sarcophagus rests the dried skeleton of a human adventurer, a leather satchel still strapped to its chest.*

- **Encounter**: 3 Tomb Skeletons (if sarcophagi are disturbed).
- **Explorer's Kit Loot**:
  - 2 × *Potion of Healing* (2d4+2 HP).
  - 18 gp, 7 sp, 100 ft silk rope, thieves' tools.
  - Journal Scrap: *"They were drawn to the stones like moths. We thought we could take them. We were wrong."*
  - **Stone of Echoing Vitality**: Minor relic (Bonus Action: heal 1d6 HP and gain +1 to next saving throw once per day).

### Room 3 — Queen's Vault

A colossal chamber lit by glowing Ethium crystal clusters embedded in the rock.

> **Read Aloud:** *The cavern expands into a towering vault dominated by a massive cluster of raw, glowing Ethium stones. Clinging to the far wall like a giant armored scorpion is a colossal Kruthik Queen, her red compound eyes fixing upon you as hundreds of tiny young crawl over her chitinous back.*

- **Encounter**: **1 Kruthik Queen / Elder** + 2 Young Kruthiks (starting guards).
- **Four-Phase Queen Boss Fight**:
  - *Phase 1 (Full HP)*: Queen hangs back on high walls, spitting acid while Young engage.
  - *Phase 2 (HP $\le$ 40)*: Queen shrieks, spawning 2 additional Young Kruthiks from wall holes.
  - *Phase 3 (HP $\le$ 30)*: Queen enters melee range, making 3 attacks per turn with razor mandibles.
  - *Phase 4 (HP $\le$ 20 — Final Push)*: Queen becomes enraged, gaining Advantage on all attack rolls.

## Encounters & Creature Stats

### Swarm & Brood

| Creature | AC | HP | Attacks & Traits |
|----------|----|----|------------------|
| **Young Kruthik** (×5–8) | 12 | 9 | Claws +4 (1d4+2). *Pack Tactics* (adv. on attack if ally is within 5 ft). *Keen Smell*. |
| **Adult Kruthik** (×2) | 14 | 22 | Multiattack: 2 Claws +5 (1d6+3) OR Acid Spit +4 (1d6+2 acid, range 30 ft). *Keen Smell*. |

### Boss Lair

| Creature | AC | HP | Attacks & Traits |
|----------|----|----|------------------|
| **Kruthik Queen / Elder** | 16 | 55 | Multiattack: 2 Mandible Scythes +6 (2d6+4) + 1 Tail Spike +6 (1d8+4). **Acid Spray** (Recharge 5–6: 15-ft cone, 3d6 acid, DC 13 DEX half). **Enrage** at $\le$ 20 HP. |
| **Egg Cluster** | 10 | 5 | Destroying prevents 1d2 young from hatching. |

## Treasure & Rewards

- **Ethium Stones**: 2–3 unrefined Ethium Stones embedded in the queen's egg mass (requires DC 12 Sleight of Hand / DEX check to extract cleanly without cracking eggs).
- **Stone of Echoing Vitality**: Relic found in the crypt (Bonus Action: heal 1d6 HP + 1 save boost once/day).
- **Explorer Coins**: 18 gp, 45 sp, 2 × *Potion of Healing*.
