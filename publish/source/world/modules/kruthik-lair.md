# Module — Kruthik lair

**Status:** Ready to run (optional side). Named option after the pool for stones / wealth / rune-axe tease. Parties may choose deeper Ethium (east cave-in) instead — both are valid.

**Canonical PDF:** [`ethium_full_session_module_v2.pdf`](../../../table-assets/pdfs/ethium_full_session_module_v2.pdf) → `/dm/pdfs/ethium_full_session_module_v2.pdf` (sections 11–17: Kruthik rooms, tombs, queen, egg stones)

Map filename spelling: **Krutick** / Tower C.

---

## Overview

Tight **3-room mini-dungeon** with an Aliens feel: skittering, eggs, walls and ceilings, queen boss. Ethium stones attracted a brood; Garnel can mark the site from rumour overheard while captive (Hammers / Gang of Four), not a first-hand map.

**General Kruthik behaviour (PDF):** climb, burrow, attack in pairs, pack tactics, retreat through small tunnels; drawn to stones and nest around them.

> [!NOTE]
> **DM Secret — Plateau Tunneling**: The rampant tunneling of kruthiks and Grey Burrowers across the plateau is the actual cause of the eastern pool collapse (DC 12 check to spot clawed side-tunnels while digging).

---

## Areas

### Room 1 — Swarm tunnels

Walls pocked with holes; dust from ceiling; skittering. Tunnels only **~2.5 ft** wide (brood movement); larger passages deeper imply adults/queen.

**Encounter:** 6–8 Young Kruthiks total. Start with **5** on the board; bring **1–2** more in round 2 if needed. Climb walls, attack in pairs, emerge from cracks — not a line fight. Dial reinforcements down if pack tactics spike.

### Room 2 — Brood chamber

Pale eggs in wet strands; larger chitin shapes among brood.

**Encounter:** **2 Adult Kruthiks** + **3–4 Young**; or 2 adults only if the party is worn down. Adults defend eggs/young, pack tactics, skitter away when hit.

**Egg pressure:** after 2 rounds, or when an area attack hits eggs, **1d2 young hatch**. Egg cluster: **AC 10, HP 5** — destroy to prevent later hatch.

### Optional side — Tombs / dead explorers

Low chamber; broken coffins; newer bodies webbed among ancient dead. Optional undead: **2–3 skeletons** or one stronger tomb guardian if disturbed.

**Fallen explorers’ kit:** 2 healing potions (2d4+2; one may shimmer blue), 18 gp, 7 sp, rope / mundane gear, journal scrap, **Stone of Echoing Vitality**.

Journal: *"They were drawn to the stones like moths. We thought we could take them. We were wrong."*

### Room 3 — Queen chamber

Huge chamber; egg clusters among blue-lit stones; queen on far wall with young crawling over her.

**Encounter:** **1 Kruthik Queen/Elder** + **2 Young** at start; more may hatch as drama requires.

**Fight flow (PDF):** Phase 1 — queen hangs back, spikes/acid while young engage · Phase 2 — brood spawn · Phase 3 — queen closes, climbs, multiattacks · Phase 4 at ~**20 HP** — final aggressive push.

**Abilities (qualitative from PDF):** multiattack, acid spit, brood spawn, terrifying chitter — escalate; don’t dump every ability at once.

**Reward:** 2–3 Ethium stones in egg mass. Extraction: DEX/Sleight DC 12 or careful plan; failure may hatch 1 young; removing multiple stones escalates risk.

The pool/Kruthik PDF gives **counts, tactics, egg AC/HP, and queen phasing**, but **no full Young / Adult / Queen AC–HP–attack lines** in that pack. Use open monster rules for numeric blocks; table cues below are from the PDF.

| Young count room 1 | start 5; up to 6–8 total |
| Adults room 2 | 2 (+ young) |
| Queen room 3 | 1 + 2 young start |

| Creature | AC | HP | Attack | Notes |
|----------|----|----|--------|-------|
