# Combat encounters (Book 1)

Resolve with your open D&D rules (or a simplified single-player procedure).  
Ids match `**Combat:** \`id\`` directives in [`book-01-the-road-to-ethium.md`](book-01-the-road-to-ethium.md).

Suggested default: party of **Thorn** (fighter), **Derek** (cleric), **Dave** (wizard), **Nibbles** (rogue), **Loki** (dog companion — treat as a small ally or Thorn feature).

Difficulty is a rough guide for your encounter builder (Easy / Medium / Hard / Deadly relative to a low-level party).

## Defeat handling

Unless a passage says otherwise, defeat means turn to a failure ending (e.g. **110** / **111**) or a softer “captured / driven off” passage if you prefer low-lethality play for younger players.
