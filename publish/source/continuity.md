# Continuity sheet — The Ruins of Ethium

The single reference for facts that must stay the same across chapters. The **continuity editor** agent (`.claude/agents/continuity-editor.md`) checks chapters against this sheet, the style guide, and the table's session notes in the private EthiumSource repo (`publish/plotlines/kids/sessions/`). When a chapter establishes a new fact, add it here.

**Where facts come from, in order of authority:** what happened at the table (session notes) → this sheet → the published chapters. If a chapter disagrees with the table, the chapter is wrong.

## Places

- **Fallcrest**: Lowtown below the cliff, Upper Fallcrest above, a broad stair between. River **Nentir**. **Knight's Gate**, **Wizard's Gate**. The **Blue Moon Alehouse** (Lowtown, by the docks), the **Nentir Inn** (Upper Fallcrest), **Naerumar's Imports** (Orest Naerumar, tiefling), the apothecary, Lannar Thistleton's paddock, the blacksmith (unnamed woman). **Nimozaran's tower** in Upper Fallcrest: two iron guardians in the entrance hall; the **study** at the top, with the teleport circle carved in the floor.
- **The Watch Bell** rings at about ten in the evening. "After the bells" means after that.
- **The road to Ethium**: river road east → burial mounds → the waterfall (a ring of **standing stones** at the foot, first described ch. 4; path climbs behind the falls; stepping stones across the river at the top, with a goblin guard post at the lip).
- **Almenor**: Fallcrest is its northernmost town; **Sunfall** is the capital. The orcs destroyed most of Lowtown a century ago; Mara's grandfather helped rebuild a street.
- **Ethium**: the whole plateau was the city. The **collapsed shrine** (safe, healing rest). The **castle on the great hill** (north-east). The **ruined tower** (west) above the **healing pool**.
- **Under the tower**: alcove → rune chamber (where Garnel was held) → maul trap and plank → **the pool chamber** (four pillars, four stones; *Vaelum Thrae*) → the dug-out passage east → **Hall of Statues** → collapsed bridge over the **otyugh's black lake** (refuse chute to the troglodytes, unexplored) → 50 ft cliff → duergar halls → crossroads → **Viewing Table** → **Assembly Hall** → the sealed gates.

## Things and how they work

- **Ethium Stones**: pale grey, blue-veined, warm. "**Aethra Lumis**" makes one glow. The party's stones by ch. 8: three from the Hall of Statues and the **Royal Stone** from Rulden's table in the manor.
- **The Viewing Table**: a stone in the socket, an Ethium-touched item on the tray; the sphere shows the past, glitches, then shows **now, live**. The Royal Stone shows the castle on the great hill.
- **Ethium phrases so far**: ***Aethra Lumis*** (light), ***Vaelum Thrae*** (the healing pool), ***Kaelith Vorn*** (bind an item to a stone; woke Thorn's axe).
- **The healing pool**: heals once a day; four stones in four pillars. Dropping a zombie in it does not cleanse him (ch. 6).
- **The teleport circle** in Nimozaran's study: one glowing rune for Fallcrest, one **dark, unreadable rune** where Nimozaran went. The jump burned a piece of **Axirian blue glass**, which cracked; following him needs a fresh piece. The party stole a blue lantern from an Amber Heron porter (ch. 9). **The Amber Heron**: arrived four days before ch. 9, tied up at the long wharf, carved heron on the prow; porters have shaved heads with a single braid and each carries a blue lantern. Vey has a shaved head (no braid) and tattooed hands.
- **Ch. 9 details**: Hesk Morrow has been robbed twice this year; Petunia is going to Fallcrest to meet a boar. Brakk carried square lantern-stamped silver (Murgaddin kept one). The note *"The tower, after the bells. — M."* is in Nibbles' pocket. The tower lamp has been dark over a week. The iron guardians stand down at "Friends". Tobbs had sworn the circle didn't work. Garnel is sleeping at the Nentir Inn.
- **Spells that don't work**: *Sleep* does nothing to the undead (ch. 3).

**Secrets stay out of this sheet.** It lives in the public novel repo. Anything the players haven't learned at the table (who people really are, what's behind things) belongs in EthiumSource, never here.
