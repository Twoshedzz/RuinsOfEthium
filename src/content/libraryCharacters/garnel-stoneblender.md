# Garnel Stoneblender

Gnome ally. Illusion magic; Ethium lore. Rescued in the ruined tower (Session 3+).

## Backstory (prep notes)

- Grandmother **Mala Stoneblender** researched Ethium; he has scraps of her research and her discoveries into the stones
- Tried to retrieve her books from a mage guild he believed had stolen them
- Worked as porter, then scribe; found most books destroyed; recovered the only remaining one and caused a stir escaping
- Ran into the **Hammers**; they confiscated the book as ransom and forced him to work for them
- Kidnapped / held in Sunfall before ending up forced to read runes in Ethium’s ruined tower

## At the table

- Found in the tower dungeon: Hammers + orc brute forcing him to read wall runes; helps the party when attacked (may cower at first in combat)
- Helped configure the **healing pool** with Ethium stones (water from a source elsewhere)
- Thinks the pool has healing properties; research on the well needs a couple of days
- Later: may stay researching at the pool, or tip the party to a **Kruthik** nest with crystals (rumour overheard from Hammer / Gang captors)
- **Session 5 seed (if played):** if he returns to Fallcrest with the party, may recognise **Mala’s handwriting** among papers in Nimozaran’s study — soft beat that the two researchers knew each other. See table plan Beat G.
