# Sergeant Murgaddin

Dwarf veteran of the Fallcrest Guard. Grey braid, battered armour, dented helmet; dry humour; takes Fallcrest’s safety seriously.

## Campaign facts

- Met Derek in the market after Mara’s rescue (Ch. 2); keeps an eye on the party
- Sent them to the **ruined manor** Hammer cellars; rewarded them after they returned with Maelis Varn’s letter
- Explained **Gang of Four** / **Sunfall** reach north after reading the letter
- Gazetteer: commands the detachment at **Wizard’s Gate**; Bloodspear War veteran (Battle of Gardbury); drinks at the Blue Moon with Teldorthan

## Spelling

Campaign / novel: **Murgaddin**. Fallcrest gazetteer / some art filenames: **Murgeddin** — same person; use Murgaddin in prose.
