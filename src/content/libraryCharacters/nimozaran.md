# Nimozaran the Green

Elderly wizard of Fallcrest; self-styled **High Septarch**. Lives in the **Septarch’s Tower** (pale green stone; old mages’ guild seat).

## Campaign facts

- Wants Ethium Stones studied — Tobbs asks the party to retrieve them (Ch. 2)
- Mara’s courier map was destined for him
- Years collecting Ethium lore; stones waking has moved work from theory to urgency
- Plot-hook note (unconfirmed at table): *may be deceived by a mage guild* — see [plot-hooks-session-era](/dm/world/places/plot-hooks-session-era/)
- **Session 5 seed (if played):** unfinished **Transit Circle** + fused **Anchor Stone** beneath the tower (life’s work); if Garnel finds **Mala Stoneblender**’s notes in the study, that implies the two researchers knew each other before she died. Soft seed only — do not expand until revealed in play.

## Gazetteer (Fallcrest location text)

Once apprenticed to the last of the old guild mages. Guild membership now: himself and his halfling apprentice. Expects a hefty initiation fee for new members. Rituals he can teach (gazetteer): Comprehend Language, Eye of Alarm, Enchant Magic Item.
