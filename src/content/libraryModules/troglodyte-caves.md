## Overview & Dungeon Background

Branching off the eastern side of the subterranean lake cavern lies a warren of **foul natural caves** inhabited by a clan of **Troglodytes**. Unlike the organized military outposts of the Duergar, these caves represent a primitive, reptilian hunting ground filled with mud, decaying bone piles, and suffocating stench.

The warren is led by **Chief Grash**, a brute troglodyte who has bound a glowing green Ethium crystal into his bone chestpiece. The caves offer a high-risk, high-reward side delve rich in magical treasures (including **Mithral Armor**, a **Javelin of Lightning**, and a **Stone of Good Luck**).

## Key Areas & Read-Aloud Descriptions

### 1 — Stench Threshold (Approach)

The stone tunnels narrow into wet, algae-covered rock.

> **Read Aloud:** *The cool cavern air suddenly grows thick, warm, and stomach-churning. A suffocating stench of rotting reptile scales, sulfur, and stagnant sludge fills the tunnel. Scraped claw marks and crushed animal bones litter the muddy ground.*

- **DM Tip — Stench Warning**: The smell serves as an immediate environmental warning. Rushing in without precaution triggers the Troglodyte Stench trait immediately upon entry.

### 2 — Ambush Galleries

Natural rock ledges and side fissures overlooking a muddy passage.

> **Read Aloud:** *The cavern opens into a split gallery with natural stone ledges overhead. Dark water trickles down the walls. Nothing seems to move, yet the overwhelming stench is almost deafening.*

- **Chameleon Ambush**: 3–4 Troglodytes cling to high ledges using *Chameleon Skin* (+6 to Stealth, advantage on Hide).
- **Stealth vs Perception**: Troglodyte Stealth check vs Party Passive Perception. Surprised PCs cannot act on Round 1.

### 3 — Carrion Choke (Refuse Pit)

A wide cavern filled with bones, discarded gear, and rotting carcasses.

> **Read Aloud:** *The floor gives way to a shallow pit choked with bone piles and rotting hides. Hanging upside down from stalactites in the ceiling is a ten-foot, worm-like horror with writhing tentacles around its mouth and razor mandibles.*

- **Encounter**: **1 Carrion Crawler**.
- **Tactics**: The crawler drops from the ceiling onto the character carrying the brightest light source, attempting to paralyze them with tentacle strikes.

### 4 — Chief Grash's Den

A large chamber centered around a crude bone throne and a green crystal altar.

> **Read Aloud:** *At the back of a filthy cavern, seated atop a mound of stolen shields and bones, sits a massive troglodyte with dark emerald scales. Bound to his chest by leather thongs is a glowing green crystal stone. He snarls, raising a heavy stone club as four guards step from the shadows.*

- **Encounter**: **Chief Grash** + 3 Troglodyte Guards.
- **Green Stone Surge (Bloodied Feature)**: When Chief Grash drops below 18 HP ($\le 50\%$), the green crystal surges with energy. Grash regains **1d8 HP**, grows in size (+2 Strength), and gains an extra claw attack each turn.

### 5 — Hidden Treasure Cache

A dry alcove hidden behind Chief Grash's bone throne (DC 11 Investigation to spot).

## Treasure & Signature Loot

### Complete Cache
- **Mithral Armor (Scale Mail)**: Medium armor (AC 14 + DEX max 2), lightweight, imposing no disadvantage on Stealth checks.
- **Javelin of Lightning**: Magic weapon (+0). When thrown and the command word spoken, it transforms into a bolt of lightning dealing 4d6 lightning damage to all targets in a 120-ft line (DC 13 DEX half) + 1d6 spear damage to the primary target. (Recharges at dawn).
- **Stone of Good Luck (Luckstone)**: Wondrous item. Grants a **+1 bonus to all Ability Checks and Saving Throws** while carried.
- **Potions & Coins**: 3 × *Potion of Healing* (2d4+2 HP), 80 gp, 1400 sp, 2600 cp.
- **Gemstones**: Azurite, banded agate, malachite, tiger eye (worth 10 gp each).
