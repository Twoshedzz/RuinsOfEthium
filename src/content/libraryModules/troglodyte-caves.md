## Overview

**Foul natural tunnels** branching from the collapsed lake cavern (post-Otyugh) and/or reachable after the duergar district. Swampy, reptile stench, chameleon ambushes from side passages. Not another organised “city” — a warren of hunters and a chief with a green-stone trick.

**Big ideas:** smell hits before sight; isolate stragglers; treasure feels half-stolen half-hoarded; can be a **one-skirmish** stop or a deeper clear. Replayable — no party-specific outcomes baked in.

**Tone:** tense and disgusting in a kid-safe way (stench, slime, traps) — not torture horror.

---

## Areas

### 1 — Approach / stench threshold

Air turns thick and sour before the tunnel widens. Perception may notice scraped claw marks, discarded bones, or a discarded hunting trap.

**DM tip:** let smell do the warning. Parties that rush in get surprised more often.

### 2 — Ambush galleries

Side tunnels and ledges; troglodytes use **Chameleon Skin** and **Stench**. Prefer hit-and-fade over fair fights in open space.

**First contact (replayable default):** 3–4 troglodytes. Add 1–2 more from side tunnels if the party is winning too easily. Do **not** start with Chief Grash unless they push deep in one sitting.

### 3 — Carrion choke (optional)

A wider chamber where refuse piles and a **Carrion Crawler** hunts from the ceiling. Good “oh no” middle beat before the chief’s den — or skip if time is short.

### 4 — Chief Grash’s den

Filthy nest, green-stone fetish, stolen goods. Grash fights only if cornered or the warren is clearly invaded. Below half HP (~18), **Green Stone Surge** (tracker cue — surge of strength / fury; keep kid-dramatic, not body-horror).

### 5 — Treasure cache

Hidden chest / niche near the den. Award the list below when they earn a clear of the cache (or a negotiated bribe / stealth theft).

| Carrion Crawler | 13 | 35 | Tentacle +8 · Bite +4 (2d4+2) · Spider Climb · Ceiling Ambush · DC 11 CON → Speed 0 |
| Troglodyte | 11 | 13 | Attack +4 (1d6+2) · Stench DC 12 CON · Chameleon Skin |
| Chief Grash | 13 | 35 | Bite +4 · Claw +4 · **Green Stone Surge** below 18 HP |

**Conditions (tracker reminders):**

- **Poisoned:** disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks
- **Grappled:** Speed 0; can still attack; escape = Action (Athletics or Acrobatics)
- **Enlarge** *(if a duergar remnant appears nearby):* Large, adv. STR checks/saves, +1d4 melee

**Tactics (tracker):** Ambush, side tunnels, isolate targets. Stench first; chameleon to leave and return; crawler from ceiling on the loudest PC.
