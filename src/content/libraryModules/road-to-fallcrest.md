## Overview & Adventure Background

Local goblin bands near Fallcrest have grown unusually bold, staging ambushes along the **North Road** and raiding merchant traffic. In truth, these goblins are receiving indirect backing from criminal gangs operates out of Fallcrest (the **Hammers**), who pay them for stolen cargo and maps.

During the party's journey to Fallcrest, a goblin warband ambushes their wagon on the road. The goblins have recently captured **Mara**, a royal courier carrying a sealed satchel and an ancient Ethium map intended for **Nimozaran**, the High Wizard of Fallcrest. If the party tracks the goblins to their hillside cave and rescues Mara, she will vouch for them at Knight's Gate and invite them to a victory feast at the **Blue Moon Alehouse**.

**Alternate Map Layout:** If preferred, the DM can stage the final rescue inside a **ruined hillside watchtower** (ground floor + upper floor) instead of a natural cave using the ![session-01-one-pager.png](/dm/maps/session-01-one-pager.png).

## Key Areas & Read-Aloud Descriptions

### 1 — North Road Ambush Site

Dense pine woods crowd the muddy road. A fallen oak trunk partially blocks the wagon track.

> **Read Aloud:** *Dense pine forest looms over the muddy road, casting long shadows across the track. Up ahead, a giant oak trunk lies across the trail. Through the damp moss, a glint of yellow catches the light — a small pile of painted wooden coins lying in plain view.*

- **Perception / Search (DC 10)**: Realizes the "gold coins" are wooden decoys painted yellow — a classic goblin lure.
- **Encounter**: 3 to 5 Goblins (scale: 3 goblins for 3 PCs; 4–5 goblins for 4–5 PCs).
- **Tactics**: Goblins fire shortbows from tree cover using *Nimble Escape* (Bonus Action to Hide/Disengage). When two goblins fall, or if one drops below 3 HP, it flees north into the brush toward the cave trail.

### 2 — The Forest Trail & Clearing

Overturned bushes, broken twigs, and blood spots lead uphill into the rocky foothills.

> **Read Aloud:** *The narrow game trail winds steeply into the rocky hills. Scuffed footprints and torn green cloth mark the mud. Resting against a mossy boulder lies a battered iron shield, emblazoned with the crest of Fallcrest — a silver falcon over a stone tower.*

- **Tracking (DC 10 Survival)**: Confirms a human figure in leather armor was dragged uphill by small, clawed boots.

### 3 — Cave Mouth / Watchtower Approach

Wisps of woodsmoke rise from a dark fissure in the hillside.

> **Read Aloud:** *A dark cave opening gapes in the cliffside, choked with thorn bushes. The pungent stench of woodsmoke and roasting meat drifts from within. A rickety wooden lookout platform overlooks the entrance.*

- **Bored Sentry Event**: A goblin lookout rests on the platform. Every few minutes, shouting erupts from inside the cave (*"Ratbag stole the meat again!"*), distracting the sentry and giving stealthy PCs advantage on sneaking past (DC 10 Stealth).

### 4 — The Subterranean Fork

The cave corridor splits into two stone tunnels.

> **Read Aloud:** *The damp corridor divides. To the west, faint chatter and the clatter of bone dice echo down a narrow passage. To the north, heavy boot tracks lead past a suspicious layer of loose leaves on the tunnel floor.*

- **West Passage**: 2 Goblin Warriors arguing over a game of bone dice.
- **North Passage — Pit Trap (DC 12 Perception)**: A 5-foot-deep concealed pit covered with thin branches and dirt. A creature stepping on it falls for 1d6 bludgeoning damage (DC 12 DEX save to catch the edge). Goblin tracks clearly circle around the rim.

### 5 — Great Cavern (Boss Lair)

A large subterranean chamber lit by smoky torches and filled with stolen crates.

> **Read Aloud:** *Torchlight flickers across a vast cavern. Stolen merchant crates and sacks are piled high. At the far wall, bound to a heavy wooden post inside a crude iron cage, sits a young woman in a courier's tunic, her satchel lying near the cage door. Standing before her is a squat goblin boss wearing a ragged velvet cloak and brandishing a rusty broadsword.*

- **Encounter**: Goblin Boss, 2 Goblin Guards, and **Ratbag** (a giant rat the size of a hunting dog).
- **Fail-Safe / Boss Action**: If the fight turns against the Goblin Boss (HP $\le$ 5), he threatens to strike the captive unless the party lets him escape through a rear escape tunnel.

## Treasure & Rewards

### Shared Quest Rewards
- **Silver Goblin Dagger**: Fine silver-bladed dagger (worth 25 gp or usable as a +0 silver weapon against beasts/monsters).
- **Charm of Bravery**: Single-use charm allowing a player to re-roll one failed saving throw.
- **Shield of Fallcrest**: +1 AC shield emblazoned with the Fallcrest falcon.
- **Courier's Satchel**: Contains Mara's royal scroll and an ancient Ethium outer-ruins parchment map for wizard **Nimozaran**.
- **Loot Box**: 35 gp, 120 sp, 1 × *Potion of Healing* (2d4+2 HP).

## Transition & Next Steps

- **If Mara is Rescued**: She accompanies the party to Fallcrest. Knight's Gate guards wave them through, and she hosts them at the Blue Moon Alehouse → [blue-moon-fallcrest](/dm/modules/blue-moon-fallcrest/).
- **If Mara is Missed**: The party can still reach Fallcrest, but must negotiate entry with Captain Murgaddin and find another hook to wizard Nimozaran.
