# Module — Journey to Ethium

**Status:** Ready to run. Bridges manor / Fallcrest → ruined tower / pool.

**Canonical Stat Blocks:** [Session 3 Stat Blocks (PDF)](/dm/pdfs/session3_stat_blocks_landscape_a4.pdf) · **Waterfall Return Contingency:** [Session 4 DM Pack (PDF)](/dm/pdfs/Ruins_of_Ethium_Session4_DM_Pack_FULL.pdf)

---

## Overview

Overland from Fallcrest to the plateau (~two days on the road), then Tobbs’s **waterfall** climb onto Ethium, then first plateau sights before the ruined tower.

---

## Areas

### 1 — River / Road (≈ Two Days)

- **Night:** wolf attack at camp
- **Burial Mounds:** axe beak herd rushes and attacks
- **Watch:** a cloaked figure can be mistaken for a rock and flee — the party is being followed

### 2 — Waterfall Climb

Tobbs’s route: treacherous stone path **behind the falls**. If the party splits (steeper right vs longer safer path), the climb gets harder.

- Goblin archers from above; **bugbears** come down
- Sleep can stop arrow fire; ledge fights with bugbears; fleeing goblins at the top

### 3 — Plateau Approach (Set Dressing)

Collapsed **healing shrine** · distant **black dragon** · half-goblin drop · **castle on a hill** · **goblin wolf riders** on a ridge (non-combat) · head for ruined tower Tobbs marked.

---

## Encounters & Stats

### Wolves (Night)

3–4 wolves. Standard wolf stats.

### Axe Beaks (Burial Mounds)

3–5 axe beaks. Hard; Sleep can drop some.

Goblin archers + **2 bugbears**. Hard when the party is split.

| Creature | AC | HP | Attack | Notes |
